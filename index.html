Tool 02
90-Day GCC Launch Checklist
Check off items as you complete them. Progress saves in your browser session.
Launch Checklist
Overall progress 0 / 24 complete
Days 0–30 · Strategy
Days 30–60 · Compliance
Days 60–90 · Go-Live
Ongoing · Operations
Define GCC purpose Critical
Cost centre, innovation hub, or product engineering centre — this shapes every other decision.
Choose entity model Legal
WOS vs EOR vs BOT. Choose for the phase you are in, not where you want to be.
Select city Ops
Mandate, budget, and attrition tolerance first. City rankings second.
Begin MCA entity registration Legal
Takes 4–6 weeks. Start Day 1 — not after city or team decisions are made.
Appoint single execution owner Critical
One person accountable for India build. Fragmented ownership = guaranteed delays.
Begin GCC head search in parallel HR
Never wait for entity to be done. The best candidates need 12–16 weeks to close.
PAN and TAN registration Legal
Required before any financial transactions or payroll processing.
GST registration Legal
Threshold-based but advisable from the start for intercompany billing.
PF and ESIC registration Critical
Mandatory before first payroll. A missed PF registration can hold up your first hires.
Shops & Establishments Act registration Legal
State-specific. Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Kolkata each have different forms.
Transfer pricing framework with HQ Legal
Define intercompany service agreement and pricing. Not an afterthought — shapes tax structure.
DPDP Act compliance plan Critical
Consent Manager integration required by Nov 2026. Start architecture now.
POSH Internal Complaints Committee HR
Mandatory under Prevention of Sexual Harassment Act 2013. Must be operational at first hire.
GCC head confirmed or joined Critical
All subsequent senior hires should flow through the GCC head — not around them.
First 5–10 technical hires sourced HR
Parallel to compliance. Don't wait for every legal box to be checked before sourcing talent.
Physical or hybrid workspace live Ops
Leased space or co-working ready with network, security, and access controls in place.
IT infrastructure and security environment Ops
Collaboration platforms, VPN, endpoint security, data residency requirements met.
KPIs defined between HQ and GCC Ops
How will you measure success in Year 1? Delivery velocity, quality metrics, hiring milestones.
First delivery milestone agreed Ops
A concrete early win for the GCC team to deliver. Sets culture and pace for everything after.
Cultural integration plan with HQ HR
Not an HR formality. Shared rituals, joint sprints, leadership visibility — from Day 1.
Monthly compliance calendar running Legal
PF filings, TDS, professional tax, GST returns — set up automated reminders.
Salary benchmarking — annual review HR
India senior leadership comp is inflating 8–12% annually. Stale benchmarks lose people.
GCC maturity assessment — quarterly Ops
Which maturity stage are you at? Plan transition to next stage before you need it.
Agentic AI integration roadmap Ops
58% of GCCs investing in Agentic AI in 2026. What tasks will you automate first?
